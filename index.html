SERVER RULES & GUIDELINES

Abide by the realm laws to maintain high-quality roleplay and game integrity.

Players must realistically value their life during all hostile interactions. If an enemy faction holds you at gunpoint without your weapon drawn, you must comply.

RDM / VDM: Attacking, executing, or running over players without prior verbal initiation and story escalation is strictly prohibited.

Metagaming: Using external information gathered from Discord, live streams, or out-of-character (OOC) channels inside the IC realm.

Powergaming: Forcing roleplay outcomes without allowing the other party a chance to react, or abusing game engine exploits.

Combat Logging: Disconnecting or exiting the game during an active roleplay scenario or battle will result in an immediate warning or ban.

New Life Rule (NLR): Upon incapacitation and respawn, your character forgets all events leading up to their death and cannot return to the combat zone for 15 minutes.

Aggressive squad sizes are capped at 8 members during active turf wars and bank heist events. Fake hostages are strictly forbidden in major vault negotiations.
